# E02 — Executable updates are not geometric success

**Status:** historical development experience; no general superiority established.

The public measurements retain P0, P1 and P2 separately. P0 is the stable parent. P1 was a validly scored but unpromoted attempt, and its history was supplied to the later proposal. P2 changed mesh decimation from 0.25 to 0.5; it produced more triangles but four distance errors increased and the two threshold metrics improved only slightly. P0 remained retained.

**Derived guidance:** inspect the entire metric vector, compare matched products, and do not substitute mesh size or execution success for geometric merit. Keep the expectation and observed outcome separate when supplying experience to another proposal.

**Limits:** upstream reconstruction was rerun, host OS versions differed, and repeatability/reference uncertainty were not fully quantified. These records do not prove the feedback mechanism ineffective or the observed differences zero. The later Agent-led camera intervention belongs to a different writable method surface.

Failed/interrupted execution also occurred in later research. Such events do not receive invented geometry scores; their complete local diagnostics are not part of this curated public snapshot. The snapshot is not a denominator for estimating campaign success rate.
